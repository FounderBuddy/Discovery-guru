# ADR 0004 — Agents propose, people decide; enforced at the ledger

**Status:** accepted

**Context.** Pre-sales output goes into bids and SOWs. A wrong "fact" costs money and credibility.

**Decision.** Agent actors can only create proposals. Confirming, approving, deciding and resolving are
human-only and refused by the ledger regardless of caller. Quotes are re-verified at the ledger.

**Consequences.** More clicks for users; mitigated by bulk confirm in the UI. A compromised or
misbehaving engine cannot change what the team has agreed.
